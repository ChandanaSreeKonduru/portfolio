Phase 08 Challenges, Learnings & V2 Roadmap Ongoing
Challenge 1 — Hallucination on regulatory queries

Zero-shot LLM hallucinated 23% on labour law. Fixed with RAG + mandatory source citations + HITL routing at <0.85 confidence. Final rate: 4.1% — regulatory-safe across all 4 countries.

Challenge 2 — Model drift in production (month 10)

China chatbot degraded after policy docs updated without re-embedding. Fixed via weekly Python KS-test drift detection + Power BI monitoring dashboard. Zero customer-visible impact.

Learning 1 — Culture eats architecture

Daily deploys required 6 months of QA trust-building + automated regression tests. The technical change was 20% of work. The cultural change was 80%.

Learning 2 — Technical debt compounds per market

Couldn't serve Nigeria's wage laws or China's data residency without REST migration first. Every new market on a monolith multiplies cost exponentially.

V2 · Agentic multi-step workflows

Replace single-turn chatbot with agentic orchestration: "approve leave + update payroll + notify team lead" in one instruction. REST APIs already support this with no architecture changes.

V2 · Predictive classifiers

Train ML classifiers on 12 months of attendance data to predict absenteeism 2 weeks ahead. Directly addresses fintech payroll exposure flagged in Nigeria + Mexico discovery interviews.

V2 · Salesforce-native integration

Embed BioTime KPIs into Salesforce CRM — sales reps see prospect workforce data gaps during discovery calls. Closes the CRM ↔ product data loop.
